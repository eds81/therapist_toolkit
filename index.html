Study Rationale

Why This Toolkit?

Large gaps in care. More than 50% of youth with detected suicide risk receive no follow-up care (Kim et al., 2019).

Structural & attitudinal barriers. Cost, insurance, waitlists, stigma, and negative beliefs all block access to treatment (Kodish et al., 2020).

Therapist hesitancy. Many therapists feel unsure, uncomfortable, or fearful about doing this work (Schmidt et al., 2016; Singer et al., 2011).

Therapist burnout is real. Therapists doing this work report high exhaustion and emotional demand, and need more support (Villarreal-Otalora et al., 2023).

"Burnout is challenging. It's very hard seeing people in really difficult places over and over, especially when your entire caseload looks like that." — Rise therapist
The Toolkit

Four Core Sections

Built from therapist-led co-design sessions, the toolkit organizes support into four interconnected areas — each designed to be practical, "grab-and-go," and grounded in evidence.

Treatment Planning / Roadmap

An interactive visual map that guides therapists through a structured 12-session arc. Click any phase to expand it and see sample evidence-based interventions for that stage of treatment.

Phase 1: Assess & Understand
Sessions 1–4
  • Suicide Risk Assessment & Safety Planning
  • Case Conceptualization & Treatment Plan
  • Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS)
  • Stanley-Brown Safety Planning Intervention
  • Crisis response planning
  • Means restriction counseling
  • Family/caregiver psychoeducation
Phase 2: Train & Teach Skills
Sessions 5–8
  • Teach and Practice Skills
  • DBT skills: distress tolerance & emotion regulation
  • CBT cognitive restructuring techniques
  • ACT and mindfulness exercises
  • Somatic grounding interventions
  • Coping card development
  • Behavioral activation
Phase 3: Consolidate & Next Steps
Sessions 9–12
  • Relapse Prevention
  • Linkage to Care
  • Termination
  • Warning sign identification & review
  • Updated safety plan review
  • Warm handoff to ongoing care provider
  • Family/caregiver transition support
  • Termination rituals & strengths review

Research Process

Discover. Design. Test.

This toolkit is being co-designed through a rigorous, community-engaged research process with Rise therapists — not built for them, but with them.

01

Discover

Focus groups and interviews with 10 Rise therapists, analyzed across 9 domains covering strengths, challenges, interventions, and toolkit vision.

02

Design & Build

Two two-session co-design workshop sequences with Rise therapists, creating and refining toolkit prototypes based on real needs.

03

Test

Mixed-method pilot testing through interviews and surveys with community therapists to assess feasibility and acceptability.
